# DriverGuid Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 21, 2026

DriverGuid helps you study the Washington State Driver Guide and prepare for the knowledge test. We built it so your study data stays with you. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and what choices you have.

## Summary

- **No account.** DriverGuid does not ask you to sign up or log in, and it never asks for your name, email address, phone number or location.
- **Your progress stays on your device.** Study progress, streaks, exam results and spaced-repetition (FSRS) scheduling data are stored only on your iPhone and, if you use them, on your paired Apple Watch and in widgets.
- **No tracking.** We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
- **We never sell your data.** We do not collect your personal study data, so we have nothing to sell or share.
- **Apple processes payments.** Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment card or billing details.

## Information stored on your device

To make the app work, DriverGuid saves the following data **locally on your device**:

- **Study progress:** answers, mistakes, favorites, readiness scores, and spaced-repetition (FSRS) review schedules.
- **Activity history:** practice and exam results, daily streaks, and session statistics.
- **Preferences:** language choice, appearance, reminder times, your planned exam date if you enter one, and other settings.

This data is kept in the app's private storage and in a shared App Group container. The App Group lets DriverGuid's home-screen widgets, Live Activities and Apple Watch app show your progress. The developer has no access to this data, and none of it is sent to us.

**Device backups.** If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, Apple may include the app's local data in that backup under Apple's own privacy terms. DriverGuid does not run its own cloud sync or server.

**Apple Watch.** If you use the DriverGuid watch app, your progress is sent directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. It never goes through our servers.

## Subscriptions and payments

DriverGuid Premium is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's In-App Purchase system (StoreKit). Apple handles all payments, billing, renewals and refunds.

- We **never** receive or store your credit card number, billing address or Apple Account credentials.
- The app asks the App Store only whether your subscription is active, so it can unlock Premium features. That check happens on your device.
- You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in **Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions** on your device.

Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Network connections

DriverGuid works offline. It connects to the internet only in these cases:

- **Question bank updates.** On launch, the app may check a public GitHub file for a newer version of the practice questions and download it if one is available. This download contains only question content and sends none of your data.
- **Official Driver Guide.** If you choose to download the Washington State Driver Guide, the app fetches the PDF directly from the Washington State Department of Licensing website.
- **App Store.** Subscription purchases and status checks go through Apple.

Like any website, those servers (GitHub, the Washington State Department of Licensing and Apple) may record standard technical details such as your IP address and the time of the request. Their own privacy policies apply. DriverGuid does not send any identifier, study data or personal information with these requests.

## Notifications

If you turn on study reminders, DriverGuid schedules them **locally** on your device with Apple's notification system. We do not use push-notification servers. You can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

## Analytics and tracking

DriverGuid does **not** include third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or attribution SDKs. The app does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.

The statistics and "analytics" screens in the app are calculated on your device from your own study history. Only you can see them.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and usage reports. Those reports are governed by Apple's privacy terms and cannot identify you to us.

## Children's privacy

DriverGuid is meant for people preparing for a driver's knowledge test, including teens getting ready for an instruction permit. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by contacting us, please let us know and we will delete it.

## Your choices and data deletion

All of your data is under your control:

- **Reset progress:** you can clear your study progress from within the app's settings.
- **Delete everything:** deleting DriverGuid from your device (and from your Apple Watch) removes all of the data it has stored.
- **Notifications:** you can manage reminder permissions in iOS Settings.

We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing to export or delete there. This applies to everyone, wherever you live, including residents of California (CCPA/CPRA) and the European Economic Area and United Kingdom (GDPR).

## Contacting us

If you send us a support email, we will receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and will not add you to any mailing list.

## Not affiliated with the Washington State DOL

DriverGuid is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the Washington State Department of Licensing or any government agency.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how DriverGuid handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy can be sent through the support link on DriverGuid's App Store page.
